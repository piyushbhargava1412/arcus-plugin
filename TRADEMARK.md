## Modified redistributions

If you distribute a modified version of ARCUS:

1. Give it **your own name** (see above).
2. Keep the `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` files intact, per Apache-2.0 §4.
3. State prominently that your version is modified and is **not** the official ARCUS
   distribution — Apache-2.0 §4(b) already requires the modification notice.
4. Do not use the ARCUS logo.
